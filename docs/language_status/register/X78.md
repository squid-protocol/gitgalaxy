- **What IBM says** (CICS TS 6.x, EXEC CICS DELAY, https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/cics-ts/6.x?topic=summary-delay, read 2026-11-10). INVREQ (condition 16): RESP2 4 hours out of range, 5 minutes, 6 seconds, 22 milliseconds. HOURS 0-99; MINUTES 0-59 with HOURS, SECONDS or MILLISECS, 0-5999 alone; SECONDS 0-59 with others, 0-359999 alone; MILLISECS 0-999 with others, 0-359999999 alone; INTERVAL(hhmmss): "mm and ss are in the range 0-59" (no range for hh).
- **Modelled.** `ggcics_taskcontrol.c` `GGCDLAY` and `CicsTask.delayRange` / `delayOrInvreq`, which the det translator calls. The command is not performed, as a planned fault is (X72).
- **What we assume.** The order when more than one value is out of range; the page gives none.
- **Status:** ASSUMED for the order only; the RESP2 values and ranges are IBM's text. Oracle-unbacked (GnuCOBOL has no CICS).
